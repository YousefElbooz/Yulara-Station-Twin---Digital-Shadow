# Textures

All textures in this folder are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are released under CC0 (public domain).
Downloaded 2026-11-17, 1k JPG (colour, OpenGL normal, roughness).

| Set | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| red_sand | desert outside the fence | https://polyhaven.com/a/red_sand |
| gravelly_sand | ground inside the fence | https://polyhaven.com/a/gravelly_sand |
| rock_boulder_dry | Uluru and Kata Tjuta on the horizon | https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry |
| asphalt_02 | Yulara Drive (road south of the plant) | https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02 |

None of these are photos of Yulara; they are generic textures chosen to match its ground.
No Esri imagery is used as a texture.
